Diastereomeric Pair Discrimination — D/L-Phe Pairs

542 held-out test pairs with identical composition and connectivity, differing only in D-/L-Phe configuration at one or two stereocenters. ΔB = BD − BL; pairwise accuracy checks whether sign(ΔB̂) = sign(ΔB), with ties counted as incorrect. Because 64.0% of test pairs have ΔB > 0, the majority-class accuracy is 0.640, so Δ AUC (chance = 0.50) is the primary metric and rows are ranked by it. Bold denotes best per column.
