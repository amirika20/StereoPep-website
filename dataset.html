Dataset Design and Generation

StereoPep

Regression Retention Time (%B)

StereoPep contains 48,788 synthetic peptides produced via high-throughput flow synthesis and experimentally characterized by reversed-phase liquid chromatography retention time. The dataset spans 11 peptide libraries ranging from 6-mers to 15-mers, with amino acid identities varied at designated positions. To reflect conventional proteomics workflows, peptides were synthesized with either arginine- or lysine-terminated sequences, mimicking tryptic digestion products.

Peptides were then derivatized with optically pure L- or D-phenylalanine at the N-terminus and, for lysine-terminated peptides, additionally at the side-chain Nε-amine. These modifications generate matched diastereomeric pairs differing at one or two stereogenic centers while preserving atomic composition and connectivity. Phenylalanine was chosen as the tag because it produced the largest and most consistent retention-time shifts among the canonical amino acids tested.

Peptide-spectrum matches were identified with FragPipe/MSFragger and filtered for confident assignments. Retention times were normalized to %B (acetonitrile fraction at elution) to provide a consistent scale across chromatographic gradients. Each peptide is also encoded as a canonical isomeric SMILES string reflecting the predominant protonation state at the experimental pH of 3, with stereochemistry explicitly encoded at every stereogenic center so that diastereomers map to distinct molecular graphs.

Benchmark Tasks

In addition to retention-time prediction, StereoPep provides three structured pair benchmarks that ask whether a model correctly predicts which peptide of a pair elutes later. Pairs are restricted to |ΔB| ≥ 1.0 %B to exclude small differences attributable to experimental variability.

Task Hugging Face config Size What it evaluates
Retention-time prediction StereoPep 48,788 peptides Regression of absolute %B. Metrics: Pearson, Spearman, Kendall, R², RMSE, MAE.
Diastereomeric pairs diastereomer_pairs 542 test · 8,339 train+val Identical amino acid identities and connectivity, differing in D-/L-Phe configuration at one or two stereocenters. Sensitivity to stereochemical changes.
Single-residue substitutions point_mutant_pairs 6,942 pairs Equal-length peptides differing at exactly one amino acid position. Sensitivity to sequence perturbations.
Terminal tag effects terminal_tag_pairs 1,549 pairs Matched peptide backbones with and without Phe tagging. Isolates the effect of terminal modifications on retention.
Canonical-only control natural 26,199 / 2,910 / 1,851 Train / val / test subset containing only canonical amino acids, used to test whether StereoPep's difficulty is driven solely by D-Phe.

For pair tasks, ΔB is the signed retention shift between the two members. For diastereomeric pairs, ΔB = BD − BL; a positive value means the D-Phe form elutes later. Models are trained to regress absolute %B, and pair metrics (pairwise accuracy, Δ-correlations, Δ AUC) are computed post hoc from the predicted differences.

Data Format

The main StereoPep configuration has one row per peptide with the following columns:

Peptide,B,M,Z,Length,SMILES
fVATTGEDWK,38.21,1292.63,2,10,N[C@H](Cc1ccccc1)C(=O)N[C@@H](C(C)C)C(=O)...
FVATTGEDWK,37.05,1292.63,2,10,N[C@@H](Cc1ccccc1)C(=O)N[C@@H](C(C)C)C(=O)...
  • Peptide — One-letter amino acid sequence. Lowercase f denotes D-phenylalanine; uppercase F is L-phenylalanine.
  • B — Elution point as percent acetonitrile (%B). The regression target.
  • M — Observed monoisotopic mass (Da).
  • Z — Precursor ion charge state.
  • Length — Number of residues.
  • SMILES — Canonical isomeric SMILES at pH 3 with explicit stereochemistry at every stereocenter.

Pair configurations contain the sequence, SMILES, and %B of both members plus a signed delta_B column (for example Sequence_f / Sequence_F, B_f / B_F for diastereomer pairs; Position, Residue_1 / Residue_2 for single-residue substitutions; Tag for terminal tag pairs). The example values above are illustrative.
